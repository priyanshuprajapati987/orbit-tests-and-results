The next 15 years will see **four simultaneous revolutions**: AI reaching human-level cognition, quantum computers becoming practically useful, biotech rewriting human biology, and India emerging as a top-3 global tech superpower. ### Key Breakthroughs Coming
- **Base editing**: More precise than CRISPR-Cas9 — correct single-letter DNA changes without cutting

- **AI-designed proteins**: Targeted drug delivery, enzyme replacement, cancer treatment — designed from scratch by AI
- **Whole-body gene delivery**: The key unsolved problem for longevity therapies. If solved → anti-aging goes mainstream
